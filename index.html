Research

Chest X-ray lesion detection with a foundation model

JLK · 2024 – now

Semi-supervised methods didn't help on our in-house data, so I looked at the representation instead. Switching the backbone to a chest X-ray foundation model helped, but lesion and normal features were still mixed. A patch-level k-NN check showed lesion regions behave differently, and I designed a module to push that signal further.

TRIEDSemi-supervised SOTA → no gain
CHANGEDCXR foundation model as backbone
ADDEDModule that separates lesion features

Malignant calcification detection in mammography

JLK · hospital deployment

The hospital wanted malignant calcifications, not every calcification. Filtering the existing model's output wasn't enough to remove benign ones, so I retrained it around malignant cases and combined predictions across the four views.

Chest X-ray screening for health check-ups

JLK · deployed nationwide

Detection for pulmonary fibrosis, cardiomegaly, arterial calcification and scoliosis. We built it with feedback from radiologists, went through clinical evaluation, and the models are now used across screening centers in Korea. After launch I retrained the fibrosis model with cases from the field.

Few-shot classification with importance-based CutOut

Sogang University · SAC 2025

Fine-tuning on a handful of images kept washing out what the pretrained model knew. We hid the regions the model relied on most during training, and added a contrastive loss so features from the same class stay close.

ContextAug: less reliance on background

Sogang University · team project

Models often recognize objects from what's around them. We counted which classes appear together, pasted rarely co-occurring objects into new scenes, and the model's attention moved back onto the object.
